How to Prevent Storm Damage Repair Problems

Taking preventive measures can help you avoid costly water damage issues down the road. Here are our expert recommendations:

  • Extend downspouts and slope the soil so runoff moves away from the foundation
  • Branches break in high wind, so keep nearby trees pruned away from the roof and siding
  • Clean out downspouts and gutters regularly, which lets heavy rainfall drain away as it should
  • Ice dams form when attics run warm, so check that yours has enough insulation and airflow
  • For insurance purposes, keep a yearly set of pictures showing the state of your property
  • Schedule a roof inspection every year so weak spots are repaired before storm season

Warning Signs for Storm Damage Repair

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Here are the most common indicators that it's time to call a professional:

  • Damaged or displaced flashing around roof penetrations, valleys or edges
  • Attic or wall insulation that is damp or matted down following a storm
  • Ceilings or walls that stain while it is storming or soon afterward
  • Damp, earthy odors that appear a few days after the storm
  • Rain blown in around door frames, window frames or openings in the wall
  • Gutters or downspouts torn loose, bent or dumping water against the house
  • Missing, damaged or lifted shingles you can see from the ground
