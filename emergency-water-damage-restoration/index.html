When to Call for Emergency Water Damage Restoration in Newark

Catching these issues early helps you avoid costly problems and protect your property. Here are the most common indicators that it's time to call a professional:

  • A growing pool on the floor is heading for the next room or running downstairs.
  • A ceiling is sagging or bulging with trapped water above it.
  • A leak upstairs is sending water down the walls of the level beneath it.
  • You see water escaping around a light fitting, from a wall outlet or through the ceiling.
  • You came home to water and do not know how long it ran.
  • The water is from a sewer backup or looks dark and smells bad.
  • The water keeps running, and the shutoff valve is out of reach or nowhere to be found.

How to Prevent Emergency Water Damage Restoration Problems

A little prevention goes a long way in avoiding expensive restoration emergencies. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • Wrap plumbing in cold, unheated parts of the house so a hard freeze cannot crack it.
  • Locate the main shutoff valve now, and show each person at home how to turn it off.
  • Keep the number of an emergency restoration company saved in your phone before you need it.
  • Aging supply lines to toilets, faucets and washing machines should be swapped out before one fails.
  • Place leak sensors near the water heater, washing machine, dishwasher and under sinks.
  • Store valuables and important papers off the floor, especially in basements and lower levels.

Why Emergency Water Damage Restoration Is Not a DIY Job

The visible water is the easy part of an emergency. It is knowing where the rest of the water went. Baseboards, wall cavities and the space beneath flooring all take on water in minutes. With a thermal imaging camera and moisture meters we locate that hidden water and treat it the same night, before it turns up weeks later as an odor or a stain.

Truck-mounted extractors, commercial-grade air movers and dehumidifiers matched to the room come with the crew, and no household keeps those around.
